# Hi, I'm Gift Nnko

I'm a full-stack developer and health informatics engineer based in **Dar es Salaam, Tanzania**. For the past six years I've been building systems that help governments and health programs collect, understand, and act on data, because better information systems mean better health outcomes for real people.

I lead a team of developers at [HISP Tanzania](https://hisptanzania.org), where we build and maintain DHIS2-based health information systems deployed across East and Southern Africa.

---

## What I work on

- **Health information systems** — DHIS2 implementations, custom apps, and data pipelines for ministries of health across Africa
- **Public health data portals** — making health data accessible and meaningful to the public, not just analysts
- **Climate and health** — exploring how climate data can improve disease surveillance and prediction
- **IoT and connected systems** — LoRaWAN-based tracking and sensor solutions using LilyGO/ESP32 and ChirpStack, from firmware to cloud dashboards
- **Open source tools** — reusable components, utilities, and frameworks for the DHIS2 ecosystem
